About the speed test

Quick: up to 8 MiB of test data in total, with at most 6 seconds of measurement each way.

Longer: up to 64 MiB in total, with at most 8 seconds each way. Stops when either limit is reached. Network overhead is additional.

A small sample can underestimate a fast connection. The result is an estimate, not a guarantee of torrent speed. Local link speed is a separate ceiling for Ethernet; Wi-Fi link rates are less predictive.

A real test contacts Cloudflare and reveals your IP address. Cloudflare privacy

This prototype uses simulated traffic only.
