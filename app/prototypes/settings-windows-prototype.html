Documented, rather than estimated

Microsoft recommends SettingsCard and SettingsExpander for app settings, immediate application of ordinary changes, switches for binary choices, and radio buttons for short mutually exclusive sets. The Toolkit's current template specifies a 68-DIP minimum card height, 16-DIP padding, a 20-DIP icon, a 12-DIP description, and a separate action column. This browser prototype uses those metrics; browser rendering is not native WinUI.

Inferred from your screenshots

An 8-pixel gap joins sibling cards visually; a larger gap and a section heading change the subject. A right chevron means navigation, a down chevron means disclosure, and Edit means a compound transaction. Icons mark group headers or independent settings. Every setting inside a group has equal alignment; no setting doubles as a heading. Home summarizes common destinations rather than repeating every preference. The specific grouping here is a design proposal, not a published Microsoft algorithm.

Native controls

Use the stock WinUI 3 Expander with its default template. Its header contains the subject and summary; its content holds standard settings controls. Period headers distinguish when from what: days, time range and duration above; the shared limit preset and its current caps below. Start, End and the Remove button share one content row. Remove is outside the header. The native time controls use a 24-hour clock.
